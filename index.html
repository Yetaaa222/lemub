Who We Are

About Lemub Group

Lemub Group Pty Ltd is an innovative company involved in asset maintenance and optimisation, business advisory, and compliance and risk management.

We specialise in the trades, engineering and scientific capabilities that keep critical facilities—such as dairy manufacturing plants and water treatment infrastructure—maintained, calibrated, and operating at peak efficiency while reducing water and energy consumption.

Resource Efficiency Targeted audits to lower facility water and energy footprints.

Capabilities

What we do

Click any service below to explore our technical scope, methodologies, and operational deliverables.

  • We provide scheduled preventative maintenance, emergency diagnostics, and mechanical optimisation for heavy processing machinery. This includes servicing dairy pasteurisers, pumps, valves, and automated production lines to eliminate unexpected downtime and extend operational lifespans.

  • Precision calibration for industrial sensors, temperature probes, pressure transducers, and flow meters. Ensuring your plant instrumentation complies with strict regulatory standards and maintains absolute batch-to-batch measurement accuracy.

  • Comprehensive physical, chemical, and microbiological water analysis. We test parameters such as pH, turbidity, total dissolved solids (TDS), hardness, and pathogen presence to ensure water supplies meet safety and processing requirements.

  • Expert selection, supply, and dosing optimisation of water treatment coagulants and flocculants. We help industrial plants and municipal systems achieve rapid suspended solids removal, improved clarification, and lower chemical operating costs.

  • Robust sanitation and disinfection systems tailored for agricultural operations, livestock watering, crop irrigation, and dairy washdown hygiene. We design reliable chemical dosing and UV treatment setups to protect livestock health and product quality.

  • Site-wide facility water balance assessments mapping every intake and discharge point. We identify hidden leaks, evaluate wastewater reuse potential, and implement targeted reduction strategies that lower both water bills and energy consumption.
